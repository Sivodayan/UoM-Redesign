# UoM-Redesign

A modern, responsive redesign concept for the official website of the **University of Moratuwa**.

Visit the live site: [https://sivodayan.github.io/UoM-Redesign/](https://sivodayan.github.io/UoM-Redesign/)

---

## Features

- **Fully Responsive Layout**: Seamless experience across mobile screens, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide desktop monitors.
- **Modern Navigation**: Sticky frosted-glass header with hamburger mobile menu toggle, quick search, and interactive Faculties dropdown.
- **Continuous Campus Gallery**: Smooth, looping marquee gallery showcasing campus imagery with hover-pause functionality.
- **Faculties & Academic Sections**: Card-based showcases for Engineering, Architecture, Business, Medicine, Information Technology, and Graduate Studies.
- **Leadership & Staff Directory**: Dedicated showcase for university officials and faculty deans.
- **Newsletter & Contact Hub**: Integrated quick links, social media channels, and email validation.
